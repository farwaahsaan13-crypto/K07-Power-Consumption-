How K07 consumption is calculated

Incoming = OGF#3 GENSET K07 + OGF#1 HESCO K07 + SOLAR-1 + SOLAR-3
Outgoing = MAIN ACB-01 + MAIN ACB-02
Losses = Incoming − Outgoing
Compressor share = 35% × (K07 COMPRESSOR + COMPRESSOR-1 + -2 + -3)
K07 = Outgoing − P/H ATS (K09) + Losses + Compressor share
